    How this is worked out

    Your portfolio value is Sure's daily balance history for the accounts you picked (sampled weekly or monthly for long periods), added together. Holdings and cash both count.

    Contributions and withdrawals come from the accounts' transactions: transfers from accounts outside your selection, and rows that read like deposits or withdrawals. Dividends, interest and fees stay inside the return. Transfers between the accounts you picked cancel out. If an account starts part-way through, its opening balance counts as a contribution.

    Time-weighted return (TWR) measures the investments themselves: each stretch between two balance readings gets a return (Modified Dietz, weighting each flow by how long it was invested), and the stretches are compounded. Your deposit timing does not affect it, so it is the fair number to put next to an index.

    Money-weighted return (MWR, XIRR) is the single yearly rate that turns your actual deposits and withdrawals into today's value. It includes your timing: adding a lot just before a fall lowers it.

    Benchmark replay buys the benchmark with your starting value and with every contribution on the day you made it, and sells it on every withdrawal, so the "would have become" figure uses exactly your cash flows.

    Benchmark data is calendar-year total returns, spread evenly through each year. It is bundled with the app, approximate, and editable below. Nothing is downloaded.
